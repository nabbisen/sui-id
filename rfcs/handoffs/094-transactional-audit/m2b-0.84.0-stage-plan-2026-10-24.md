**Written.** 2026-10-24 JST, by the architect.
**Authorized.** `0.84.0` is M2b's settings area, S01–S09, wave 2 before
wave 1 — `@nabbisen`, 2026-10-24: *"Authorized."* on
[`m2b-scope-and-0.84.0-2026-10-24.md`](m2b-scope-and-0.84.0-2026-10-24.md).
**Governing RFC.** [RFC 094](../../accepted/094-transactional-audit-registry.md),
**Accepted**. M2a closed 2026-10-03.

## A — one thing the scope proposal did not know, found while planning

**S08 cannot be made atomic without also fixing a second defect**, and
that defect is worse than the audit gap the wave is for.

`pending_change::apply` consumes the pending row — which commits — and
returns the decrypted payload. The caller then applies the setting.
`handlers/settings.rs:671-710` shows what that means for SMTP: after the
consume has committed, the handler parses `tls_mode` and **returns
`BadRequest` if it is unknown**, reads the existing config, seals the
password, and only then writes.

So today: **a pending change can be consumed and the setting never
applied.** The row is one-shot, so the administrator's queued change is
gone and nothing was changed. No audit gap is involved — this is a
correctness defect in its own right, and S08's inventory row already says
its surface is *"`consume` **and setting mutation**"*, so the inventory
has been describing the intended shape all along.

**Making S08 atomic therefore means restructuring the seam**, not
converting a call: validate and seal **before** the transaction (RFC 094
keeps crypto outside it), then one transaction containing consume,
binding check, setting write, and the audit event. The binding check has
to move inside too, because today it runs *after* the consume has
committed.

That is a design, not a mechanical conversion, and it is why S08 gets a
stage of its own below rather than riding along with S07 and S09.

## B — the stages

### Stage 1 — S07 and S09, the two single-row commands

`create` and `cancel`. Each mutates one row and emits one event; neither
has a payload to apply or a caller to restructure.

**This is where M2b's conversion pattern gets established**, and that is
the stage's real purpose. M2a's commands had their mutation and audit
already adjacent; these do not — the mutation is in `sui-id-store`'s
`pending_settings_change` repo and the audit is a best-effort append in
`sui-id-core`'s `settings/pending_change.rs`. The sealed command has to
live in the store crate, with core calling it. Getting that seam right on
two simple commands is worth more than doing it quickly on five.

Events: `settings.pending_change.created`,
`settings.pending_change.cancelled` — the names already in use, so the
audit stream does not change shape for an operator reading it.

### Stage 2 — S08, with the restructure §A describes

The hard one. Its design is written when stage 1 lands and the seam is
known, not now — writing it against an un-built seam is how the stage 4
design went wrong on RFC 095.

Two events, not one: `settings.pending_change.applied` and
`settings.pending_change.binding_failed`, the second with
`AuditResult::Failure`. Both must commit with the consume.

**One behaviour to decide, not inherit, when that design is written:**
today a binding failure still consumes the row. That may be right — it is
one-shot and refusing to burn it invites retries — but it is currently a
consequence of the ordering rather than a decision, and after the
restructure it becomes an explicit choice.

### Stage 3 — S01–S06, sealing what is already atomic

Six commands that already append inside their transaction
(`server_settings.rs:145`, `:180`, and siblings). No defect to close; the
work is the typed descriptor, the sealed command, the moved caller, and
the inventory row.

Cheap *because* it is already correct, and last because it blocks nothing
and teaches nothing the earlier stages have not.

## C — what `0.84.0` does not contain

Wave 4 (client administration, nine best-effort sites — `0.85.0`), wave 3
(federation configuration, three `target-legacy` rows and one
`target-absent` depending on unsettled RFC 096 scope), the authority
switch (RFC 094 §M2b item 6, which must come last), and S10, which is
bootstrap rather than settings administration and was excluded by name in
the scope.

**`0.84.0` does not close M2b.** Two of four waves and the authority
switch remain after it.
