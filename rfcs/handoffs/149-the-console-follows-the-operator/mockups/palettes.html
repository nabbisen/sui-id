Two palette directions, both within RFC 144's rules

A refines today's palette (purple accent, warm greys). B is calmer (indigo accent, slate greys; info moves to blue so it stays apart from success). In both, all text is at least 7:1 and every boundary at least 3:1, measured against both page surfaces. Status is never carried by colour alone: every badge and banner has its word. Look for: which reads more calmly and more clearly to you, in light and in dark.
