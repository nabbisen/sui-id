# RFC 129 — owner review

**Reviewer.** `@nabbisen`, project owner. **Not the author** — the architect
wrote RFC 129 — so this is the first genuinely independent design review in this
line of RFCs, in the ordinary sense of the word rather than the one a retired
document supplied.
**Date.** 2026-10-01.
**Record.** His words: *"RFC 129 was reviewed and accepted."*

This note exists because RFC 000 requires a durable, repository-relative
reference for the review of a security-sensitive RFC, and a message in a
conversation is not one. **It records that the review happened and who performed
it. It does not characterise what he checked**, because the architect was not
present for it and will not put findings in his mouth.

If he wishes the record to say more — what he examined, or any condition on the
acceptance — this file is where it goes, and only he can add it.
